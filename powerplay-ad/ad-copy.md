## Live copy

**Primary text**

> $3.41 away from a free tee? Don't add another item. PowerPlay your cart ⚡
>
> One tap tops up the difference and the free gift drops straight in.
>
> Black Friday is up to 40% off, plus a free gift with every $50: kit bag, crew socks, free shipping, training towel, and the Essentials Tee at $250.
>
> Ends Sunday 29 November.

**Headline:** Up to 40% Off + a Free Gift Every $50

**Description:** Ends Sunday 29 November

Why: the first line repeats the video's hook word for word, so the copy and the 3-second hook land as one message. The second line explains the mechanic in a single benefit, and the third makes the offer concrete by naming the five gifts. The headline carries the offer for people who skim past the video, and the description matches every other BFCM ad.

## Alternatives to test

- **B, gift-ladder led:** "Every $50 you spend unlocks a free gift this Black Friday. Come up a few dollars short? Tap PowerPlay in your cart and it tops you up to the next one. Up to 40% off. Ends Sunday." Leads with the offer, suited to cold Prospecting audiences.
- **C, short (Reels/Stories):** "Short of a free gift? PowerPlay it ⚡ Up to 40% off + a free gift every $50. Ends Sunday." Most of the selling happens in the video itself.
- **Headline alternatives:** "PowerPlay Your Cart" (novelty) and "Free Tee at $250. Ends Sunday." (specific reward).
