# Gift angle: copy deck

"Gifts sorted for him": the MMA-obsessed loved one, bought for by girlfriends, mums, dads and sisters.
The images are in `out/gifts/`, in 4:5 for feed and 9:16 for Stories and Reels, plus a 1:1 carousel.

Character counts use Meta's guidance: primary text shows about **125 characters** before "See more", headlines are
best at 40 or fewer, descriptions at 30 or fewer. Every primary text below puts the hook in the first 125.

The offer line used everywhere is: "30% off fightwear, 25% off hoodies and tees until Sunday 29 Nov." It's accurate for 20–29 Nov. After that, see "After Black Friday" below.

## Wording changes from the brief, and why

- **"Fat couch potato" is now "couch potato" / "get him off the couch".**
  - Meta rejects ads that link fitness to body image or call out someone's weight, so "fat" is a real
    rejection risk.
  - It could also put off a buyer whose partner is bigger.
  - "Off the couch" keeps the joke without the body jab.
- **GTA 6 appears only in the `-gta` variants, as plain text.**
  - Mentioning a game by name to make a comparison is usually allowed.
  - We use none of its logos, art or fonts, and say nothing about it beyond "can wait" or crossing it off a list.
  - The risk: Rockstar or Take-Two can still report the ad and get it taken down.
  - So test it against the `-game` version, which says "another video game" and does the same job with no
    trademark.
- **"30% off fightwear" replaces "up to 30% off".**
  - Fightwear is 30% off and hoodies and tees are 25% off.
  - The Essentials Training Tee is 40% off, so "up to 30%" would be wrong.
- **No stats or quotes were invented.** None of the ads include reviews, numbers or shipping promises.

## Targeting

Let the creative do most of the targeting. Each persona ad names its buyer in the first line, so Meta finds those
people faster than detailed targeting would.

- **Where to run it:** a separate ad set, e.g. "GIFTING | BFCM", in the BFCM campaign.
- **Audience:** Advantage+ audience, with these suggestions:
  - women aged 20–45, plus interests UFC, mixed martial arts, Brazilian jiu-jitsu, boxing (partners and family
    of fans often follow the same pages);
  - parents.
- **Exclusions:** keep the existing "Purchasers 7d" exclusion.
- **What goes in it:** hero, couch vs cage (both versions), list, the four persona ads and the budget carousel.

## After Black Friday (from Monday 30 Nov)

1. Set `sale.on` to `false` in `gifts.json` and run `node scripts/render-gifts.mjs`. The prices switch to full price
   and the strip reads "Christmas gifts, sorted · cagecraftco.com".
2. In the copy above, swap the offer line for your Christmas shipping cut-off once you have it, e.g. "Order by
   [date] for Christmas delivery." The budget tiers recalculate from the full prices.
