# Merchant Center Feed Optimization

The feed decides which searches Shopping and PMax show for, and it affects quality score. It is one of the biggest levers and is under-used.

## Product titles (most important)
- The default title (e.g. "BRAND Nathan Dress Size 12 Blue") wastes the front of the title: brand means nothing on non-brand searches, product names mean nothing to people who don't know you.
- **Keywords first, brand last (or not at all if nobody knows the brand).**
- Choose keywords from the **search terms report** (what actually converted), not guesses. With no data yet, use sensible category terms and revise after 4–6 weeks.
- Add attributes (colour, material, fit, use case) while it fits; each one adds search surface.
- Example: `Formal Dress – Nathan – Blue Satin Midi – Brand`; for CPG: `Dissolvable Creatine Powder – Clear Flavoured Portable – Brand`.
- **Variant hack:** each variant is a separate listing, so give variants different titles, images and descriptions targeting different keyword pools.
- Change titles in Merchant Center with **attribute rules** or a supplementary feed, so Shopify product names stay intact.
- **Roll changes out gradually** and watch CTR, CPC and returns per listing; mass changes reset learning.

## Images
- Use a **4:5** aspect ratio; 9:16 fashion images get auto-cropped badly. Override with a supplementary feed if needed.
- The image is about 70% of the listing: treat it like a hook. Make it stand out from competitors (e.g. coloured background where others use white) while still attracting the *right* buyer, not everyone.

## Other attributes
Clean descriptions, product type, colour, size, gender, age group and material. It's a one-time job.

## Multi-currency
Use **a separate feed and price per currency/country.** Showing converted prices ("converted from AUD") kills CTR and conversion. For NZ, set NZD prices via Shopify Markets before targeting NZ.

## Promotions
Sale prices or Merchant Center **Promotions** make the discount show on the listing. Promotions need approval, so submit them several days before the sale.
