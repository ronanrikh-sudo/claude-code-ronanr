# Bidding and Budgets

## Bid strategies by stage
| Stage | Strategy | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| New account, no conversions | Manual CPC or Maximize Clicks (with a max CPC cap) | Get off these as soon as you have conversions. |
| Some conversions (about 15–30) | **Maximize conversion value** | E-commerce: use *value*, not *conversions*. |
| Stable volume | **Target ROAS** | Stability; set slightly below trailing ROAS. |

- **Expansive** (Maximize conversions / value) spends part of the budget testing new auctions, audiences and regions, which unlocks scale but is riskier.
- **Restrictive** (target CPA / target ROAS) does almost no testing when the target is tight, so it is efficient but siloed. Rotate between them over long periods so the account doesn't stagnate.
- **Value over conversions:** Maximize conversions chases the lowest CPA, so it pushes cheap products (a $40 tee) over better-ROAS expensive ones (a $200 jumper).

## Target ROAS death spiral
ROAS dips → campaign can't hit target → spend pulls back → fewer conversions → worse modelling (it uses 30–90 days of data) → ROAS dips further → the campaign dies. Fix: **lower tROAS aggressively**, not timidly.

## Budgets
- Increase budgets in **about 20% steps**; Google wants slow, methodical changes. Don't double budgets or halve target CPA overnight.
- Cutting budgets fast is generally fine.
- "Limited by budget" is an **indicator, not proof** of profitable headroom; almost every campaign shows it.
- Budget-capped campaigns can't enter every auction; before peak periods, raise budgets early and control spend with tROAS instead.

## Break-even maths
- Break-even ROAS = 1 ÷ contribution margin (after COGS, payment fees, shipping, discounts, gifts).
- Check whether revenue includes GST/tax; if so, adjust.
- Expect new-account month-one ROAS to sit below mature performance.

## Over vs under-spending (Blue Sense framing)
- **Over-spenders:** Google captures demand Meta and TikTok created, then claims high ROAS; pushing spend there isn't incremental.
- **Under-spenders:** treat Google as bottom-funnel only and cap it, even though their category has lots of cold search demand.
- Decide which camp you're in using back-end revenue, not platform ROAS.

## Measurement notes
- Platform conversions lag (7-day+). During peaks, steer by **blended ROAS / MER** (total revenue ÷ total ad spend).
- If you can't measure it, that doesn't mean it isn't working; smaller brands without MMM tools should watch engagement signals (time on site, pages per session, add to carts, email signups) for top-of-funnel efforts.
