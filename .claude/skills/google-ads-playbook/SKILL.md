## Core rules (apply everywhere)

1. **Bottom of funnel first.** Brand search + bestseller Shopping, then non-brand Shopping/PMax, then top of funnel (Demand Gen, problem search) only if the product supports it.
2. **Consolidation beats segmentation.** Every extra campaign needs a commercial reason: brand vs non-brand, geography, margin tier, or genuinely different product category.
3. **Shopping over search for e-commerce.** 80–90% of spend in Shopping; the shopper sees image and price before clicking.
4. **Exclude brand from prospecting campaigns** (brand list *and* negative keywords on PMax), or they become glorified retargeting.
5. **Judge Google on incremental back-end revenue**, not in-platform ROAS. PMax over-credits itself.
6. **Move slowly upward.** Raise budgets / loosen targets in ~20% steps; cutting fast is fine. No structural changes in Q4 after Nov 1.
7. **Know break-even ROAS before spending.** Break-even ROAS = 1 ÷ (gross margin after fees, shipping, discounts). Target comfortably above it.
8. **Never claim a guaranteed return.** New accounts have no conversion data; expect a learning period of weeks.
