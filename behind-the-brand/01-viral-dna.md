# The DNA of a Behind the Brand Video That Gets Follows

Research summary (Oct 2026), applied to CAGECRAFT. Each claim is tagged:
**[IG]** = Instagram or Mosseri said it. **[DATA]** = platform or third-party data. **[CONSENSUS]** = creator best practice, not proven.

---

## 0. How Instagram decides who sees it (the rules of the game)

- **The top three signals are watch time, likes per reach, and sends per reach.** Sends (DM shares) count slightly more for reaching *non-followers*. **[IG, Mosseri Jan 2025]**
- **Seconds watched count, not just the % completed.** A 60s video that holds people beats a 7s one they rewatch once. **[IG]**
- **New Reels go to your followers first.** If they perform, they get pushed to non-followers. **[IG]**
- **Polish is now "the tell" of AI content.** Raw, phone-shot founder footage that "only you could make" is what gets favoured going into 2026. **[IG, Mosseri Dec 2025 memo]** → Good news: your format is exactly that. Keep it raw.
- **Tools you should be using:**
  - **Skip Rate** in Reels Insights: the share of viewers who swipe away in the first 3s. This is your hook score. **[IG]**
  - **Retention chart**: shows the exact second people leave. Re-hook just before it. **[IG]**
  - **Trial Reels**: shown only to non-followers. Post the same video with 2–3 different hooks and keep the winner. **[IG]**
  - **Link a Reel / "Watch Part 2" button**: chain every series episode. Link back to Episode 1 so cold viewers can binge. **[IG, Aug 2025]**

## 1. THE HOOK (0–3s): three layers, one idea

Reels autoplay **on mute**, and the decision happens in about **1.5 seconds**. **[CONSENSUS]** Each layer has its own job:

| Layer | Its job | Rules | CAGECRAFT example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **VISUAL** | Stop the thumb with no sound | Start **mid-action** or show the **outcome first**. No intro, logo or "hey guys". Movement in frame 1. | Box slamming onto the floor of an empty stock room. Phone screen showing a UFC fighter's story wearing your rashie. Tape-gun rip on an order. |
| **ON-SCREEN TEXT** | Give the **number or stakes** the picture can't | 5–10 words, big, inside the safe zone. **Add** to the VO, don't copy it. | `$50K IN ONE MONTH — OR I'M IN DEBT` |
| **AUDIO / VO** | Open a **question** that only the ending answers | Start mid-sentence with energy. No "so…". A hard SFX (rip, slam, punch) works as an audio pattern-interrupt. | "My supplier just told me my Black Friday stock is going to be late." |

**The test:** mute it and cover the text. Does the visual alone make you curious? Then read only the text. Does it alone give a reason to stay? If both pass, the VO is a bonus.

### Hook formulas, rewritten for you

| Formula | Why it works | Your version |
|---|---|---|
| **Stakes / consequence** | Loss aversion. People stay to see if you lose. | "If this stock doesn't land by the 15th, Black Friday is over before it starts." |
| **Specific money** | Numbers stop the scroll and invite debate. | "This tee sells for $59.95. Here's how much of that I actually keep." |
| **Mistake / negative** | Vulnerable content outperforms highlights. This is backed by your own data ("Biggest upset" > "Biggest drop"). | "This mistake cost me $[X] and nearly killed the brand." |
| **Outcome first** | Shows the payoff, then makes them want the "how". | *[UFC fighter's story on screen]* "Most brands pay thousands for this. I paid zero." |
| **Day X / series** | Signals an ongoing story, so they follow to keep up. | "Day 1 of trying to do $50K in one month." |
| **Nobody tells you** | Insider status. | "Nobody tells you what happens when a UFC fighter actually replies to your DM." |
| **POV** | Puts the viewer in your shoes. It already works for you (fight night POV: 181 likes). | "POV: you're 21 and 300 orders just landed in one night." |
| **Contrarian** | Disagreement in the comments helps reach. Only use it if you genuinely believe it. | "Fighter sponsorships are a waste of money for small brands. Here's what I do instead." |

### Hook killers (avoid these)

- "Hey guys / So today…" or any greeting.
- A logo sting or slow drone shot as the first frame.
- Text that just transcribes the VO.
- Burying the stake ("…and that's when I realised the stock was late" at 0:20). Put the stake in the first sentence.

## 2. THE MIDDLE: keep them watching

- **Open one loop in the first 3s. Close it only at the end.** ("Did I hit $50K?" is only answered in the finale.) **[CONSENSUS]**
- **Re-hook every 5–8s.** That can be a new angle, location, SFX, a text pop, or a stakes update ("But there's a problem.", "$12K to go."). Your own Notion already has this rule: "Whenever the video loses interest, add SFX + B-roll / change locations mid-talking." **[CONSENSUS]**
- **Length** **[DATA]**:
  - **20–45s** for one moment (one-day stories, single updates).
  - **45–90s** only when there's a real arc (setup → twist → payoff).
  - The broadest dataset (~140k business Reels) found **45–60s** got the best engagement. Over 3 minutes, engagement dropped off.
- **Payoff in the last 10–20%, then end within ~1 second.** Long sign-offs hurt completion.
- **Loop it.** Write the last line so it runs back into the first, or end on the same frame you opened with. **[OPINION, widely used]**

---

## 3. THE CLOSE & CTA: give them a reason to come back

Series drive follows because a numbered day tells a stranger *there's a story here, and the only way to keep up is to follow*. **[CONSENSUS; TikTok names "serialised stories" as a trend signal]**

**Rules:**
1. **Tie the CTA to the open loop, not to "follow for more".**
   - ❌ "Follow for more 👊"
   - ✅ "Follow to see if the stock lands in time."
   - ✅ "Day 2 tomorrow: I find out what air freight is going to cost me."
2. **Put the CTA on screen as text in the last 2–3s while the payoff is still playing.** Don't add a spoken outro after the story has ended.
3. **Ask for sends, not likes**, because sends reach non-followers: "Send this to someone who's thinking of starting a brand."
4. **Series consistency:** use the same opening text format every episode (`DAY 7 · $0 / $50K`), a one-line recap ("Last time: supplier said the stock's late"), then a new stakes update straight away. Each episode must still make sense to someone seeing it first.
5. **Use the "Watch Part 2" link button** on every episode, and link back to Episode 1.

---

## 4. The one-page checklist (use before every script goes to VO)

- [ ] **Frame 1** works on mute: mid-action or outcome first
- [ ] **Text** has a number or stake and doesn't copy the VO
- [ ] **First spoken line** opens a question and has no greeting
- [ ] Stakes are stated in the **first sentence**
- [ ] A re-hook every **5–8s**
- [ ] The loop is closed only in the **last 10–20%**
- [ ] The CTA is tied to the loop and shown as **text in the last 2–3s**
- [ ] It's **sendable**: a mate would DM it to someone
- [ ] Length matches the story: **20–45s** for one moment, **45–90s** for an arc
- [ ] **Two alternative hooks** written for testing in Trial Reels
