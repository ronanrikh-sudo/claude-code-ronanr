## THE ANCHOR SERIES: "Road to a $50K Month"

**This is the one to start now.** You already have a real challenge with real stakes playing out in the next six weeks. BFCM starts Nov 22. That gives you ~12 episodes and a guaranteed finale. A finale also sells, because people who've watched 10 episodes want to be part of the result.

**Premise:** "I've set myself a goal to do $50,000 in one month. Stock is late, I've taken a loan to pay for it, and if this doesn't work [consequence]."

> ⚠️ Your call: how much of the loan and margin detail you're comfortable putting on the brand page. The more real it is, the harder it hits. You could say "a loan" without saying how much it was.

## FORMAT 1: One moment, one day
*"Something happened today."* Fast to make, high relatability.

1. **The canvas logo, remake.** Lead with what it *cost* you to get there: the negotiation and the moment you saw it live. Don't lead with the achievement. Hook: "This cost me [X] and [3 weeks of DMs]." `[what/where was the canvas, what did it take?]`
2. **"I walked into a gym and asked them to stock CAGECRAFT."** Film it whatever happens. A no makes a better video than a yes.
3. **"Someone I've never met just fought in my shorts."** Watch a customer/athlete fight in your gear (stream or live). Your fight-night POV format already works.
4. **"I found out a ranked UFC fighter posted my brand while I was [at uni / at work / in the shower]."** A single-moment version of the UFC story. `[where were you?]`
5. **"The first sample of the Monarch just arrived. It's wrong."** Rejected sample vs final. Show the flaw up close.
6. **"I almost cancelled this drop today."** `[a real near-miss: late stock, a bad sample, money]`
7. **"A customer messaged me this today."** A screenshot of a real DM or review that hit you, and why. `[pick one]`

## FORMAT 2: The arc (months or a year)
*"How we got from A to B."* Big payoff, works well as a pinned post.

8. **"What it actually cost me to hit 6 figures at 21."** It's not about the money. It's what you gave up while mates were doing normal 21-year-old stuff. (Your Idea Board marks identity/mindset as your best-performing category.)
9. **"Every CAGECRAFT drop, ranked from worst to best."** Pre-order → hoodies → summer → Legacy → BFCM. The mistake at each step.

12. **"I was doing a marketing degree while building this. What uni didn't teach me."** `[confirm still studying / finished]`

## FORMAT 3: 3 tips / lessons
*"3 things that helped me do X."* People save and share these, and shares count a lot for reach.

13. **"How I got 3 ranked UFC fighters in my gear with $0. Steps you can copy."** Remake of the underperformer as a teachable step-by-step. `[the actual steps: who you DMed, what you offered, how you followed up]`
14. **"3 mistakes that cost me $[X] in my first year."** Fold in the "biggest upset" story. `[the 3 mistakes + rough $]`
15. **"What a $59.95 tee actually costs me to make."** Landed cost ≈ $26, then everything people forget: ads, returns, freebies, packaging, the stock that doesn't sell. Expect people to argue in the comments, which helps reach. Your call on transparency.
16. **"Nobody tells you how hard it is to get your first 100 customers."** `[how you actually got them]`
17. **"The real reason most fightwear brands fail."** An opinion piece. Have a strong take; people will argue in the comments.
18. **"3 things UFC fighters actually care about when you send them gear."** Insider angle no one else in your niche can make.
