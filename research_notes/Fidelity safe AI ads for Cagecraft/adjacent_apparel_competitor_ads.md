# Adjacent DTC apparel brands — currently winning paid-social ads (image and video), catalogue for CAGECRAFT

Research date: 16 Nov 2026. "Days running" = start date to 16 Nov 2026. All ads below were ACTIVE in the Meta Ad Library when pulled. Scope excludes direct fightwear competitors (covered by another researcher).

**How this was gathered (read first)**
- Meta Ad Library MCP tool (`ads_library_search`): 20 calls, used mainly to resolve page IDs. It returns only the newest 50 ads per query with no body text, so it could not surface long-runners on its own.
- The public Ad Library web UI loaded in headless Chromium without login. Each listing was filtered to active ads that started on or before 15 Sep 2026 (60+ days), by page or by keyword, for AU, US and GB. Only the first ~30 cards load per query when logged out, so every listing is a sample of the top of Meta's default order, not a census.
- Every creative in the catalogue was actually viewed: the image, or a contact sheet of 8–16 evenly spaced video frames, was downloaded from the ad card and inspected. Beat timings are therefore approximate (± 1–2 s).
- **Audio was not auditioned** (no playback here). Where captions change word-by-word in sync with a speaker, a spoken voice-over is inferred and labelled as such. Music is unknown for every video.
- "Winner" proxies used, named per ad: **(L)** longevity while still active; **(D)** duplication — "N ads use this creative and text" on the card, or the same creative under several ad IDs; **(G)** reuse across countries (seen in more than one country listing); **(R)** rank in Meta's default listing order, which the UI labels "Impressions: high to low"; **(T)** third-party teardown. Meta publishes no spend or impression numbers for these ads, so (R) is a relative signal only.
- "MULTI" = the card says "This ad has multiple versions" (Meta multi-version / catalogue-fed ad); the version I saw is one of several.
- Aspect ratios are read from the served media (360×640 = 9:16; 480×600 = 4:5; 600×600 = 1:1; 450×600 = 3:4).

## For these brands, which ads have been active longest and which creative concepts are duplicated most?

### Takeaway
The longest-running active ads sit with small and mid-size brands, not the big names: Raskol (statics live since Nov 2022–May 2023), Ryderwear AU (video and statics live 347–526 days), RepJA mesh jerseys (400 days), OverRip's hoodie explainer (343 days) and Realism's 350gsm hoodie colour grid (273 days). LSKD, Gymshark, Culture Kings, Comfrt, Represent and Darc Sport cycle creative every 2–7 weeks, so for them duplication and listing rank are the only usable signals.
