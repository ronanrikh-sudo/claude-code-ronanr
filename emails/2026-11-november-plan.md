The public can't shop until Fri 27 Nov, so from Fri 20 Nov the pitch to non-subscribers becomes "join the list and get in now":
- Change the site popup and Instagram bio/stories to: "Early access is live. Join the list to shop a week before everyone else."
- Update the **Welcome — Free Kit Bag Popup** flow's first email for 20-26 Nov so new signups get the early access link straight away.

## Setup before Fri 20 Nov (Omnisend and Shopify)

- **Early access gating (decide):** a list-only discount code in the emails is simplest, but codes can leak to the public. Alternatives are a hidden collection or a customer-tag gate in Shopify.
- **Segment "Purchased in sale":** placed order since Fri 20 Nov. Exclude from #7 onward so buyers stop getting "shop now".
- **Segment "Kit interest":** clicked #2 or #3.
- **Abandoned cart / checkout flows:** make sure they don't carry a discount code that stacks with the sale. They'll run for 11 days of sale traffic.
- **Old AI drafts:** delete or hold the "[Mia] Complete Cage Kit" and "[Mia] Training Towel" drafts so they don't go out on top of this run.
- **SMS (if enabled):** one text each for early access open (#7), early access ends (#10) and final hours (#14).
